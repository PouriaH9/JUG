Design proposals for review

  • A direct mobile route to the collection without completing all animation chapters.
  • An attractive static model fallback when WebGL fails or the device is slow.
  • Shipping and returns information near the product action.
  • Dedicated product URLs if sharing and search discovery are launch goals.
